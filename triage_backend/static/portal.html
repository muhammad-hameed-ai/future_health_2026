Clinical Governance & Defense

The 5 Gates of Safety Pipeline

Strictly adhering to Inception 42 directives: "Never let the LLM do math", enforce hardcoded optical pre-filters, and mandate clinician oversight before EMR dispatch.

Gate 1

Zero-PII Privacy Guard

Patient photos never leave device RAM. Only an anonymized mathematical risk vector traverses TLS 1.3, fully compliant with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 2 of 2019.

100% On-Device RAM
Gate 2

Optical Quality Filter

Laplacian focus >3.2 rejects motion blur in <10 ms. Luminance histogram analysis blocks flash glare (L > 234) and deep light starvation before inference.

<10 ms Pre-Scan
Gate 3

Neural OOD Gatekeeper

MobileNetV3 neural classifier rejects non-skin inputs (walls, clothes, documents, user facial selfies) with 99.41% accuracy, preventing false medical alarms.

99.41% Accuracy
Gate 4

Deterministic Stolz Math

"0% LLM Math" rule. Stolz ABCD TDS and Argenziano 7-Point scores are strictly computed via hardcoded C++/Dart math. Generative AI is forbidden from score calculations.

0% Hallucination
Gate 5

Clinician Sign-Off Gate

Software as a Medical Device (SaMD Class IIa). Mandatory doctor cryptographic signature required before EMR dispatch to Malaffi. Zero unsupervised autonomous actions.
